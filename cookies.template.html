Cookie policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short

  • This website sets no cookies.
  • It stores nothing in your browser. The cookie notice appears on every visit, because we don’t remember your choice.
  • It loads no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no social media buttons.
  • The cookie notice is there to tell you this. Accepting it doesn’t switch on any tracking.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can save in your browser. Websites also use similar technologies, such as local storage, tracking pixels and device fingerprinting. In this policy, “cookies” covers all of these.

2. Cookies on this website

TypeUsed here?
Strictly necessary storageNone. The site stores nothing, not even that you accepted this notice, so the notice shows on every visit.
Preference cookiesNone. The language switch on this page is not remembered.
Analytics and statisticsNone. We don’t count or follow visitors.
Advertising and trackingNone.
Third-party contentNone. Fonts, images and scripts are all served from this website itself. No requests go to Google or other outside servers.

3. The MileZero app and web demo

The Android app uses no cookies and no trackers. The separate web demo stores two small items in your browser’s local storage: your limit and sound choice, and the fact that you agreed. They stay in your browser. Read more in the privacy statement.

4. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. GitHub does not set cookies on this website for us, and we don’t receive that data. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for how it handles it.

5. Links to other websites

The “Message the team” button opens a contact form on another website. That website has its own cookie and privacy rules.

6. Your choices

You don’t need to do anything: nothing is stored. You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser at any time. This website will keep working.

7. Changes and questions

If we ever add cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. The date at the top shows the latest change. Questions? Contact Antonio Frungillo through the contact form on his portfolio.
