Privacy statement and cookies

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short

  • MileZero reads ride offers on your own phone and colours them red or green.
  • The Android app has no internet access. Nothing it sees leaves your phone, and nothing is stored.
  • On your phone we only keep your limits, your sound setting, and the fact that you agreed.
  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking. The web demo uses two small items of local storage, described below.
  • We only receive data when you choose to send it to us: a screenshot, a recording or a note.

1. Who is responsible

MileZero is a student project by a team of Business Innovation students at Inholland University of Applied Sciences in Amsterdam. It is not a company yet. For questions, contact Antonio Frungillo, the project contact, through the contact form on his portfolio.

2. What the app does on your phone

When you tap Go online and allow screen capture, MileZero looks at your screen about twice a second. It reads the screen with text recognition that is built into the app and runs on your phone.

It looks for a ride offer and reads the amount, the minutes, the kilometres and the drop-off address. The address is only used to name the area, such as the centre or Almere, as extra information on the band. It ignores everything else, including passenger names and other apps.

Each screen picture is thrown away right after it has been read. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent. MileZero’s own band is marked secure, so it never reads itself. You can stop at any time: tap Go offline, stop the screen sharing, or remove the app.

An offer can show details about a passenger. MileZero does not use them.

3. What is saved on your phone

WhatWhy
Your limit per minute in the citySo the app can judge rides the way you decided
Your limit per kilometre between citiesSame, for long rides
Your sound alert setting (on or off)To play or skip the sound and buzz
That you agreed on the first screen, and whenSo the screen does not come back every time

This stays on your phone. To remove it, go to Android Settings → Apps → MileZero → Storage → Clear data, or remove the app.

4. What leaves your phone

Nothing. The Android app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything. When you tap “Privacy statement and cookies”, your phone’s browser opens this page. That is an ordinary visit to a web page, made by your browser and not by the app.

6. What you send us

If you choose to send us a screenshot, a screen recording or notes to help test MileZero, we receive and read them. Please cover or cut out passenger names and addresses first. We use what you send only to improve how MileZero reads offers and, without names, in our project report. We keep it only for the test round and delete it afterwards. We rely on your consent, and you can ask us to delete what you sent at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data about you, to object to its use, to take it with you, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app does not send us data, most of what it handles is on your phone and in your own hands. For anything you sent us, contact us through the link above. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

8. Uber, Bolt and their rules

MileZero only reads and shows. It never taps Accept or Decline, and you always decide. Uber and Bolt have their own driver terms and may limit tools that read the screen. Using MileZero is your own choice.

9. Changes

We update this statement when the app changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

1. Wie is verantwoordelijk

MileZero is een studentenproject van een team studenten Business Innovation aan Hogeschool Inholland in Amsterdam. Het is nog geen bedrijf. Vragen? Neem contact op met Antonio Frungillo, de contactpersoon van het project, via het contactformulier op zijn portfolio.

2. Wat de app op je telefoon doet

Als je op Go online tikt en schermopname toestaat, kijkt MileZero ongeveer twee keer per seconde naar je scherm. Het scherm wordt gelezen met tekstherkenning die in de app zit en op je telefoon draait.

De app zoekt een ritaanbieding en leest het bedrag, de minuten, de kilometers en het afzetadres. Het adres wordt alleen gebruikt om het gebied te noemen, zoals het centrum of Almere, als extra informatie op de balk. Al het andere wordt genegeerd, ook namen van passagiers en andere apps.

Elke schermafbeelding wordt direct na het lezen weggegooid. Er wordt niets opgeslagen en niets verstuurd. De balk van MileZero is zelf beveiligd, zodat de app zichzelf nooit leest. Je kunt altijd stoppen: tik op Go offline, stop het delen van je scherm of verwijder de app.

Een aanbieding kan gegevens van een passagier tonen. MileZero gebruikt die niet.

Dit blijft op je telefoon. Verwijderen kan via Android Instellingen → Apps → MileZero → Opslag → Gegevens wissen, of door de app te verwijderen.

4. Wat je telefoon verlaat

Niets. De Android-app heeft geen toestemming om internet te gebruiken, dus kan niets versturen. Als je op “Privacy statement and cookies” tikt, opent de browser van je telefoon deze pagina. Dat is een gewoon bezoek aan een webpagina, gedaan door je browser en niet door de app.
