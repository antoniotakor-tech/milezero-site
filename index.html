For Uber and Bolt drivers in Amsterdam

Red or green,before you tap.

You get about ten seconds to accept a ride. MileZero reads the offer on your own phone and checks it against the limit you set. Good rides turn green, bad rides turn red. You decide.

Start it once, then drive.

  1. 1

    Set your limit

    Choose what a minute of your time is worth, for example €0,50. Tap start and allow screen sharing.

  2. 2

    An offer comes in

    MileZero reads the fare, the pickup time and the trip time. Each screen picture is thrown away right after.

  3. 3

    The band answers

    A band at the top of your screen says Good ride or Bad ride. MileZero never taps for you.

One sum, your limit.

fare ÷ (pickup + trip minutes)

Above your limit is green, below it is red. MileZero doesn’t guess your costs or fees, because the screen doesn’t show them.

It reads the offer. Nobody else sees it.

What it reads
The fare and the minutes on the offer, on your phone, with text recognition built into the app.
What it keeps
Your limit, your sound setting and that you agreed. No rides, names or addresses.
What it sends
Nothing. Everything stays on your phone.
This website
No cookies, no analytics, no requests to other servers.

Uber and Bolt have their own driver terms and may limit tools that read the screen. Using MileZero is your own choice. Read the full privacy statement and cookie policy.

Where MileZero is now

Why we built it

Vision

Empowering drivers to maximize shift earnings through smarter choices, not longer hours.

Mission

Replacing algorithmic uncertainty with instant financial clarity in every 10-second window.
